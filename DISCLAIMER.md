# Disclaimer

**LexBG is a research tool. It is not legal, tax or accounting advice, and it does not create a lawyer–client relationship.**

- Texts are reproduced from official sources (parliament.bg, EUR-Lex, and consolidated texts you import from official websites), but parsing can fail, sources can be incomplete, and **a stored text may not include the latest amendments**. LexBG reports this as `text_status` and in the `warning` field of every result — always check it.
- The only authoritative publication of Bulgarian law is the **State Gazette (Държавен вестник)**; for EU law, the **Official Journal of the European Union**.
- Promulgation dates are not entry-into-force dates. Transitional provisions, thresholds, exceptions and deadlines must be read in full.
- Topic packs and `compliance_checklist` are a **map of areas to research**, not a determination that an obligation applies to you.
- Obligations produced by `extract:obligations` are generated by a language model. Their quotes are verified verbatim, but the *interpretation* (who / must / deadline) can be wrong until a person has reviewed it.
- If you use LexBG through an AI assistant, the assistant's answer is the assistant's interpretation. Check the cited provisions yourself and consult a qualified professional (адвокат, счетоводител, данъчен консултант) before acting.
