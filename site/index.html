Why it exists

A law in force is the act plus every change to it

General AI answers Bulgarian legal questions from memory: plausible, often outdated, sometimes citing articles that don't exist. Checking it is hard because most amendments are not where you would look. They sit inside other laws: a paragraph in the VAT Act changes the Accounting Act, the budget law changes the tax code.

What LexBG does differently

Three rules every answer follows

„…“ чл. 12, ал. 1

Verbatim, never paraphrased

Provisions come straight from the official text with a Bulgarian-style citation and a source link. A citation checker catches references that don't exist.

original

Honest about what is current

Every law is marked current (consolidated), original (as first adopted, later changes not applied) or missing, and that warning travels with every answer.

what_changed(ЗСч, чл. 38)

Changes come first

For any law or single article: every amending act, including amendments buried in other laws, with the State Gazette issue and the amending text.

Use cases

Who it helps

Founder or freelancer Имам ЕООД с двама служители и онлайн магазин. Какво трябва да спазвам?

A map of the areas that apply — accounting, VAT, employment, consumer law, the euro changeover — with the first provisions to read and the questions to ask an accountant.

Accountant Какво се промени в Закона за счетоводството от 2024 г. насам?

Every amending act since that date, including changes made through the euro law and the budget law, with the exact amending paragraphs.

Compliance at a bank or fintech Кой е действителен собственик по ЗМИП и коя директива стои зад него?

The legal definition in § 2 от ДР на ЗМИП, the EU directive it implements, and DORA, AMLR and MiCA in Bulgarian.

Citizen Мога ли да се откажа от потребителски кредит?

The right and the deadline from чл. 29 ЗПК — 14 days — quoted word for word, plus whether that text has changed since.

Developer building legal AI Провери цитатите в този договор.

A citation checker and verbatim retrieval you can put under any model. Search quality is measured on real questions and the weekly build refuses to publish if it drops.

How it works

From the State Gazette to your assistant

  1. Official sources

    Every act the National Assembly adopts (parliament.bg) and EU acts in Bulgarian from the Publications Office.

  2. Weekly build

    Acts are parsed into articles and §, amendments are linked, tests and search checks run, then the database is published.

  3. On your machine

    A 6 MB SQLite database, downloaded once and updated in the background. Nothing you ask leaves your computer through LexBG.

  4. Your AI assistant

    Claude or any MCP client calls 10 read-only tools and answers with quotes, citations and warnings.

Install

Set it up in a minute

You need Node.js 22.13 or newer. Nothing to clone or build: the first start downloads the database (about ten seconds), later starts take a second.

Settings → Developer → Edit Config, add this, then restart Claude Desktop and start a new chat.

{
  "mcpServers": {
    "lexbg": {
      "command": "npx",
      "args": ["-y", "https://github.com/StanK1/lexbg-mcp/releases/latest/download/lexbg.tgz"]
    }
  }
}

One command in your terminal.

claude mcp add lexbg -- npx -y https://github.com/StanK1/lexbg-mcp/releases/latest/download/lexbg.tgz

For clients that run a command; the database is kept in a volume.

docker run -i --rm -v lexbg-data:/data ghcr.io/stank1/lexbg

Recommended: add the lexbg-research skill so Claude follows the full method. Every tool is described in the tool reference.
